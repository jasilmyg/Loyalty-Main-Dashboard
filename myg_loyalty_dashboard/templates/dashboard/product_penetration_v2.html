Product Penetration · V2 Report

Customers who bought a Mobile but not AC, TV, Washing Machine or Refrigerator — and how many of them converted in each period

Choose a period and press Apply…
How it is counted: Database = customers who bought a Mobile on or before the period end and have not bought a Washing Machine, Refrigerator, TV or AC on or before that date. Purchased Count = number of customers from the database at the start of the period who bought any of AC / TV / Washing Machine / Refrigerator during the period (each customer is counted once, in the period of their first such purchase). Purchased Value = sold-price total of those customers’ AC / TV / Washing Machine / Refrigerator purchases in the period. Quarters are calendar quarters (JFM, AMJ, JAS, OND). Monthly shows the last 6 months, Quarterly the last 4 quarters, Yearly the last 3 years.
